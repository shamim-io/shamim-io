## What I do

- 🩺 **Clinical AI on patient data**: models on electronic health records, developed and validated together with clinicians.
- ✅ **Validation**: how clinical AI fails when its evidence is outdated, biased or incomplete, and how to test for that before deployment.
- 🛠️ **Software that runs**: production cloud infrastructure, CI/CD and full-stack development in industry; live, open-source research software now.
